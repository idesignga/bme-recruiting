Coach E

My name is Coach Goines, owner and CEO of BME Recruiting. I an a Linebacker Specialist, training athletes from youth through college.
Atlanta-born and Florida-raised, I played at South Cobb High School under Coach Shane Queen as both a linebacker and running back before continuing my career in professional indoor football. After a minor knee injury, I shifted my focus to giving back to the game. I founded my 7v7 Program and recruiting service to help athletes sharpen their skills, gain exposure, and create real opportunities at the nexl level.

About Coach BK

Brian BK Wilkinson is a Defensive Back Specialist, training kids from 7u to the Pros. BK was born in Atlanta, Georgia and played corner at Westlake High School. He went on to play at Ventura College in Ventura, CA as well as Campbellsville University in Campbellsville, KY. After college, he had a couple of NFL workouts before injuring a hamstring. Shortly after, he came back to Atlanta and started BK Total Fitness and his coaching career.
